## Notes for the Reviewer

- The mutation table in `reports/T-008-mutations.txt` was produced against a
  **copy** of `src/` in the session scratchpad with `node_modules` symlinked;
  the repo's `src/flightManager.ts` was never edited (`git diff --stat` empty,
  `git status --porcelain` shows no `src/flightManager.ts` entry).
- Two mutants survive and are argued equivalent there: the inclusive
  `deviationNm <= ARRIVAL_RADIUS_NM` edge (unconstructible without an exact
  10.000000 nm coordinate, which design §10.5 forbids and which
  `ARRIVAL_RADIUS_NM` offers no override for), and dropping the `state ===
  'FLYING'` half of `onCrash`'s guard (redundant with `endFlight`'s own
  `currentFlightId === null` early return).
- Two tests exist purely to kill mutants the acceptance list does not name:
  `duration_sec is rounded to the nearest second, not truncated` (5500 ms →
  6 s) and `breaks an exact cross-track tie towards the earlier segment`
  (three waypoints on one parallel produce a bit-for-bit tie, and `d <
  bestCrossTrackNm` keeps the earlier segment).
