lines as follows (grouping is mine; every individual assert() still runs and

   one `stopUplink` call recorded.

   `app.crashed`, clicks `[data-lsk="R5"]`, asserts one `restartSidecar` call.

   recorded `setConfig` call's `args[0]` carries `ingestToken === token` plus
