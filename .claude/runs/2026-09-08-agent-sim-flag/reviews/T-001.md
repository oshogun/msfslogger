Non-blocking follow-ups:
- Top-level `/home/guilherme/msfslogger/README.md` §"MSFS 2020 vs 2024"
  (lines 222–231, outside this task's `allowed_paths`) still tells the reader
  to "change that line to `Protocol.SunRise`" in `agent/agent.js` — this is
  now stale and should be updated to point at the `--sim` flag in a follow-up
  task. Flagged by the Dispatcher too; confirmed real by direct read of the
  file. Not blocking since it's out of scope for this task's `allowed_paths`.
- `node agent.js --sim` (flag given with no following value, argv exhausted)
  silently falls back to the 2020 default rather than treating it as an
  error, because `parseSimArg` returns `undefined` in that case and
  `resolveSimProtocol` treats `undefined` the same as "flag omitted".
  Confirmed by running `node agent.js --sim` — it printed `Connecting to
  SimConnect (Protocol.KittyHawk)...` and looped retrying, no error. Not
  covered by the acceptance criteria (which specify a recognized-but-invalid
  value, e.g. `--sim xbox`, not a missing value) and is a defensible reading
  of "unrecognized value," but worth a one-line fix if the team wants
  `--sim` with a dangling flag to fail loudly instead.
- `-s=2024` (short flag + `=`) works as an unlisted side effect of the
  parser (`arg.startsWith('-s=')` branch) — harmless, undocumented, not worth
  a criterion.
