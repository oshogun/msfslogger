(The two import lines are abridged with `...` in the middle for width only; the
full lines are in the file and in `git diff`. Everything else is verbatim.)

**One deliberate departure from §5.4's pseudocode, wire-invisible.** §5.4 puts
step 7 (`res.json(...)`) *after* the `try`. Taken literally that double-sends,
because the `catch → 500` arm does not `return` and execution would fall through
into step 7 — the same reason both neighbouring handlers
(`app.patch('/api/planned-legs/:legId')`, `app.put('/api/flights/:id/planned-leg')`)
put their success `res.json()` **inside** the `try`. This handler follows the
file's idiom. Every status code and body in §5.3 is unchanged by the placement,
as the matrix in §3 below shows.
